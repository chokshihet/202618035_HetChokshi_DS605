Pipeline Implementation Details

Task 1: Data Scraping
    
    Fields Extracted: Title, Category, Price, Star Rating, Availability, Product Description, UPC, Number of Reviews, and Product URL.  

    Extraction Scope: Extracted 100 complete records across 5 distinct catalog pages. 

    Pagination Logic: The spider navigates item detail links sequentially per catalog page and automatically follows next pagination links until reaching the designated record limit.    


Task 2: Preprocessing and Feature Engineering
    
    Data Cleaning: Stripped redundant whitespace from text fields, mapped textual star ratings (One through Five) into standard integers (1 through 5), parsed stock counts from availability strings, and converted currency strings into float values. 
    
    Deduplication: Identified and removed duplicate entries based on unique Universal Product Codes (UPC).
    
    Feature Engineering: Created four engineered attributes:
        1. description_word_count: Numerical word count extracted from product descriptions.  
        
        2. price_band: Categorical binning of book prices (Budget, Mid-Range, Premium) using quantile splitting.
        
        3. value_score: Numerical ratio defined as Rating/Price.

        4. Recommended: Boolean flag denoting items with Rating >= 4 and Price < Median Price.


Task 3: Visualization and Analysis
    
    Generated Visual Outputs:
        Price Distribution (plots/price_distribution.png): Histogram and kernel density estimate highlighting price spread.  
        
        Rating Distribution (plots/rating_distribution.png): Count plot showing the frequency of 1-star through 5-star ratings.
        
        Average Price by Category (plots/avg_price_by_category.png): Horizontal bar plot displaying top high-cost categories.
        
        Price vs. Rating (plots/price_vs_rating.png): Box plot evaluating price variations across star rating tiers.
        
        Description Word Cloud (plots/wordcloud_descriptions.png): Visual depiction of high-frequency keywords across product descriptions after removing stop words.  


Task 4: Insights and Interpretation
    
    Key Empirical Findings
        1. Price Distribution: Book prices range uniformly between £10.00 and £60.00, with a mean price of approximately £35.00 across the sample.
        
        2. Rating Balance: Star ratings are evenly distributed across all 1 to 5 rating levels without skew.
        
        3. Price vs. Rating Relationship: Box plot analysis demonstrates that price bears no direct correlation to star rating. Premium-priced titles receive low ratings at similar rates as budget-tier titles.
        
        4. Category Pricing Patterns: Specialized categories (such as Travel and Politics) average higher per-unit prices (>£40.00), whereas general categories present lower mean costs.
        
        5. Inventory Levels: Stock counts range between 1 and 22 units per title, maintaining an average inventory level of 10–12 items per listing.
        
        6. Value Identification: The engineered value_score metric identifies 5-star titles priced in the lower quartile (£10–£20) as providing the highest relative consumer value.  
        
        7. Description Word Cloud: Word cloud visualization shows recurring narrative themes centered around keywords such as world, story, life, family, and novel.
